Essences & items

Every player on Remi carries an essence. It gives you a passive while you hold it and four moves. Here's what each one does, and what every custom item is for.

How essences work

Hold your essence in your main hand or offhand to use it.

Getting one

When you join for the first time, a ritual gives you a random essence from the 15 base ones. It's bound to you, so it can't be dropped or stored. Use /essence to swap between the ones you own.

Tiers

Every essence starts at Tier I with moves 1 and 2. An Essence Upgrader takes it to Tier II (the max): it unlocks F and Sneak + F, adds 10% damage and makes the passive stronger.

Sparks

Sparks are your lives. You start with 5 and can hold up to 7. Dying drops 1 spark, and every spark you hold means more damage and shorter cooldowns. Hit 0 sparks and you're banned for 30 minutes. Use /withdraw to turn sparks into items.

Custom items

These are the only custom items on Season 2. Recipes are on the home page.

Spark

A piece of someone's life force. Right-click it to absorb it and gain +1 spark (up to 7).

Only comes from players (when they die or withdraw) and from the Rejuvenator. It can't go in chests or ender chests.

Essence Upgrader

Raises your equipped essence to Tier II: unlocks F and Sneak + F, +10% damage and a stronger passive.

Crafted. See recipe

Essence Rejuvenator

Restores your sparks to 5. It does nothing if you already have 5 or more.

Crafted. See recipe

Revival Book

Brings back a player who hit 0 sparks. They return right away with 1 spark. Right-click to pick who to revive.

Crafted. See recipe

Essence Token

Rerolls you into a random essence you don't own yet and equips it. You keep every essence you unlock forever, and your sparks stay the same.

Crafted. Once you own all 15, your next token gives you the Origin.

Mythic Essence drop

A glowing, unclaimed mythic that drops where the boss died. Hold right-click for 5 seconds to claim it. Getting hit by a player stops the claim.

From the Wither or the Warden. See mythics

The Origin

The 16th essence, and the rarest. The Origin is all of the essences combined into one, and its moves call on every one of them at once.

  1. Collect all 15 base essences using Essence Tokens. Each token gives you one you don't own yet.
  2. Once you own all 15, use one more Essence Token.
  3. The token gives you the Origin and equips it.

Passive (while held): Strength I (II at Tier 2), Speed II, Fire Resistance

Mythic essences

Two new essences for Season 2, dropped by the hardest bosses. They aren't limited, so anyone who gets the drop can claim one. When someone claims a mythic, the whole server is told.

Aurex has a 10% chance to drop when the Warden dies.
