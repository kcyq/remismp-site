The Origin sleeps

You found the beginning. Seven riddles guard what's inside. Every answer is a number, and some are longer than others. Write them in order, with nothing between them, and the ten digits will wake it.

  1. Fifteen were handed out freely. One was born from all of them. Two were never given at all, only torn from the dead. When the codex is opened, how many voices answer?

  2. What the Rejuvenator restores is not the limit. Steal from the fallen until your soul can bear no more, then count your lights at the brim.

  3. Three skulls, one body, raised from soul sand. Slay it a hundred times. How many of those deaths leave crimson behind for someone to claim?

  4. Forged from the core of a thing made of wind, it falls from the sky to crush. The world allows only a handful to ever exist. Count the handful.

  5. When steel meets flesh, an unseen chain binds you to the world. Break it too soon and you return a corpse. How many seconds does the chain hold?

  6. It cannot be hidden in wood or in the End's dark chest. It cannot be broken. It hatches nothing, yet it makes its keeper harder to kill. By how many hearts?

  7. The right hand wakes the first move, the bent knee the second. The rest sleep until the essence climbs. Name the step at the summit, with nothing above it.
